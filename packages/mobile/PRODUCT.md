# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual readers who pick up books for leisure during commutes, before bed, or in quiet moments. They want a warm, inviting reading experience that makes books feel accessible, not academic. They may not be tech-savvy. The app should feel like a friendly companion, not a productivity tool.

## Product Purpose

ReadPal is a mobile reading companion that combines EPUB reading with AI-powered conversation. It helps readers understand, engage with, and enjoy their books through contextual AI chat, reading streaks, and personalized companions. Success means readers look forward to opening the app, feel guided without feeling managed, and discover more in their books than they would alone.

## Brand Personality

Playful, Curious, Friendly. The interface should feel like a well-read friend who's excited to discuss what you're reading. Inviting, never intimidating. Warm colors, approachable type, gentle motion. Delight in small moments of discovery.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with corporate blue/gray palettes and data-heavy layouts.
- Social media feeds with notification spam, engagement metrics, and comparison-driven UI.
- Kindle/Apple Books clones that treat reading as a sterile file management task.

## Design Principles

1. **Reading is pleasure, not work.** Every screen should feel like opening a book, not checking a dashboard. Warmth over efficiency.
2. **Companion, not teacher.** The AI guides and suggests, never lectures. Conversational tone, optional depth.
3. **Small discoveries, big delight.** Streaks, persona interactions, and reading insights should surface as gentle surprises, not demanded attention.
4. **Accessible by default.** Dyslexia-friendly options, adjustable typography, high contrast modes, and reduced motion are first-class features, not afterthoughts.
5. **Own your reading identity.** Don't mimic Kindle's sterile library or a social feed's noise. Be the cozy bookshelf with a friendly cat on it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA target with enhanced readability features: dyslexia-friendly font options (OpenDyslexic, Literata), adjustable line spacing and margins, high contrast and sepia reading modes, full reduced motion support, large touch targets, and screen reader compatibility across all interactive elements.
